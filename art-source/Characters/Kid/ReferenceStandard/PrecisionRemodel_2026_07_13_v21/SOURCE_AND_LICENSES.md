# M1 Kid source and license record

Recorded: 2026-08-29

## Scope and factual boundary

This record applies to the Kid character whose retained M1 source masters are
under `PrecisionRemodel_2026_07_13_v21`, whose M1 visual-authoring outputs are
under `A2_VisualRework_2026_08_29`, and whose three runtime derivatives are
published as `public/models/characters/kid.glb`, `kid-lod1.glb`, and
`kid-bootstrap.glb`.

The Kid geometry descends from an Output generated by Tencent Hunyuan3D-2 and
subsequently remodeled, UV-authored, rigged and material-authored in this
project. M1 did not call the Hunyuan service again and does not distribute
Tencent model weights or code. The fixed agreement has territory,
distribution, downstream-use, provider-disclosure and MAU conditions. The
facts are recorded here; this file is not a legal approval for public release.

## M1 derivative chain

1. `tools/art_pipeline/build_a2_kid_visual_rework.py` opens the retained rigged
   v21 master. It preserves topology, UVs, the exact joint names/hierarchy and
   skin weights while deterministically broadening the head/hair and hoodie
   shoulder read and strengthening the backpack and shoe accents.
2. The same script bakes `Kid_v20_NativeBodyHead.BodyPalette`, an existing
   project-authored POINT-domain semantic vertex-colour layer, into
   `A2_VisualRework_2026_08_29/Textures/Char_Kid_A2_Semantic_BaseColor_2K.png`.
   No external texture, prompt, reference image or generative service is used
   in this A2 texture pass. The frozen A2 texture is 1,187,176 bytes with
   SHA-256 `9fde5298a0487a1e56eea3aebf9bf6f17e40888cb35eafacb81986adf5bc8bbc`.
3. `build_web_character_animation_sets.py` retargets the twelve product-trunk
   clips to the unchanged skeleton. `apply_character_pbr.py`, the native
   gltfpack/Meshopt pass, KTX2 pass, LOD1 builder and bootstrap builder then
   create the three runtime tiers and their reports.

Final A2 Blend, runtime GLB, report and screenshot hashes are recorded in the
M1 Kid delivery report after the artifacts are frozen; this source record does
not copy provisional hashes into a supposedly final table.

## Quaternius gameplay animation source

The authoritative Kid contract remains exactly:
`Caught`, `EscapeCelebrate`, `HideEnter`, `HideExit`, `HideIdle`, `HidePeek`,
`Idle`, `Interact`, `Run`, `TurnLeft`, `TurnRight`, and `Walk`.

They are project retargets/refinements of Quaternius Universal Animation
Library 2.0 Standard, licensed CC0 1.0. The pinned extracted source GLB is
6,671,104 bytes, SHA-256
`1b7bf67866360665426bb99e4c71bd619f19b408453c24e30f0c3071601eee5c`;
the source archive is 14,541,205 bytes, SHA-256
`18ff1a7215f4852b320203e8aaf02a1578b5c8eef9027fbaedfcedc7b85a3ac2`.
See `docs/licenses/QUATERNIUS_UNIVERSAL_ANIMATION_LIBRARY_CC0.md`.

M1 does not rename or delete those clips, add a Crouch mechanic, change the
E/Q/C control contract, or alter the gameplay FSM. The four Hide clips remain
separate because they are gameplay-critical transitions, not a visual-only
loop.

## Rebuild and release gates

Every final Kid derivative must preserve:

1. one shared 21-joint skin, each joint name occurring exactly once;
2. fewer than 2% zero-weight vertices and non-zero LeftHand/RightHand coverage;
3. +Z runtime facing, feet at Y=0 and unit authored scale without a runtime
   correction patch;
4. all twelve exact gameplay clip names;
5. Meshopt + KTX2 delivery, the high/lod1/bootstrap derivation reports and the
   frozen first-play loading protocol;
6. the public-release legal block until the product owner resolves the
   Hunyuan conditions and Concept 01 provenance.
