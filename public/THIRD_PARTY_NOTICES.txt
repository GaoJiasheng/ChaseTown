Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 — generated Kid and Villain geometry
-----------------------------------------------------------

The Kid and Villain characters use geometry descended from Outputs generated
with Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0. The game does not ship Tencent model weights,
model code, or a hosted Hunyuan inference service.

Official agreement snapshot:
  https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/f8db63096c8282cb27354314d896feba5ba6ff8a/LICENSE

Notice required by Section 3(d) of the official agreement:

Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 is licensed under the Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 Community License Agreement, Copyright © 2025 Tencent. All Rights Reserved. The trademark rights of “Tencent Hunyuan” are owned by Tencent or its affiliate.

Modification notice:

The generated geometry was substantially modified for Chasing. Both characters
were reshaped in Blender, cleaned and normalized on retained 21-joint armatures,
simplified and compressed, converted to KTX2 runtime materials, and retargeted
to the game's separately licensed gameplay animations. The Villain additionally
uses a cropped MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 nose-and-mouth relief and project-specific
material maps. The Kid A2 pass preserves project-authored semantic vertex
colours as its clean runtime BaseColor zoning. Runtime high, LOD and bootstrap
derivatives are modified files; they are not unmodified Tencent model outputs.

Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2.0 — Police animations
----------------------------------------------------------------

The Police character's five gameplay clips are modified and retargeted from
Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2.0, Standard edition, released under
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0):

  Universal Animation Library 2.0 · Standard.zip
  Bytes: 14,541,205
  SHA-256: 18ff1a7215f4852b320203e8aaf02a1578b5c8eef9027fbaedfcedc7b85a3ac2

  AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.glb
  Bytes: 6,671,104
  SHA-256: 1b7bf67866360665426bb99e4c71bd619f19b408453c24e30f0c3071601eee5c

Official source:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html

OpenGameArt source and license record:
  https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library

Canonical CC0 legal code:
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

CC0 applies the waiver, fallback-license, limitation, and disclaimer terms
summarized in the MakeHuman section above. Attribution is not required; this
voluntary notice preserves the author, edition, hashes, and modification chain.

The source motions were retargeted to the project's 21-joint skeleton, stripped
of runtime root motion, adjusted for contact and transitions, and published as
the Police gameplay clips Idle, Run, Alert, Interact, and Resolve. The original
download archive and source animation GLB are build inputs and are not
distributed with the game.
