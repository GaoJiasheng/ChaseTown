Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 — generated Villain geometry
---------------------------------------------------

The Villain character uses geometry descended from an Output generated with
Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0. The game does not ship Tencent model weights, model
code, or a hosted Hunyuan inference service.

Modification notice:

The generated geometry was substantially modified for Chasing. The project
reshaped the body and garments in Blender, replaced the prior detached face
overlay with a cropped MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 nose-and-mouth relief, authored new
project-specific material maps, cleaned and normalized skinning on the retained
21-joint shared armature,
simplified and compressed the mesh, converted textures to KTX2, and retargeted
the game's separately licensed gameplay animations. Runtime derivatives and
their bootstrap variants are modified files; they are not unmodified Tencent
model outputs.
